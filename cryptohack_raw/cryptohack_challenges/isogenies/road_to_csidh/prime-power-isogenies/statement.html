For SIDH, we used a prime of the form p = 2 e A ⋅ 3 e B − 1 p = 2^{e_A} \cdot 3^{e_B} - 1 p = 2 e A ​ ⋅ 3 e B ​ − 1 and we computed isogenies of degree ℓ e \ell^{e} ℓ e by finding a point of order ℓ e \ell^{e} ℓ e and using this as a generator.

The characteristic for CSIDH primes is instead a product of many small primes with exponent equal to one. To compute a ℓ e \ell^{e} ℓ e isogeny in CSIDH, one should instead compute a point of order ℓ \ell ℓ , then compute the codomain of the isogeny generated by this point and repeat the whole process e e e times.
