Galois Counter Mode (GCM) is the most widely used block cipher mode in TLS today. It's an "authenticated encryption with associated data" cipher mode (
[AEAD](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Authenticated_encryption)
), yet not resistant to misuse.
  
  
See
[here](https://toadstyle.org/cryptopals/63.txt)
for a great resource on the inner workings of GCM, as well as this attack.
