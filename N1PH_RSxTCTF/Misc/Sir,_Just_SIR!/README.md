## 📝 Description

Amar - an investigative agent - has been tracking Rolex, the notorious kingpin of the drug underworld and leader of the criminal syndicate known as Vetty Vagaiyara.
After months of surveillance, Amar discovers that the syndicate has recently brought an official online operation back to life. At first glance, the site appears to be nothing more than a legitimate storefront, but Amar suspects that something much deeper is hidden behind it.
Every discovery seems to lead to another trail, another identity. What initially looks like a simple web investigation gradually turns into a hunt across the syndicate's digital infrastructure.
Follow the trail. Uncover what syndicate has been hiding. Track down the people behind the operation, piece together the evidence, and ultimately find the key that Amar needs to bring the investigation to an end.
Can you step into Amar's shoes, follow the trail, and recover the ultimate key?
