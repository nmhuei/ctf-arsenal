## 📝 Description

The old empire hung a gerege from a rider's belt and every gate let them
pass. UB-CENTRAL kept the idea: a bearer proves, in zero knowledge, that
their tablet carries the Khan's authority - the tablet itself never crosses
the wire, only a seal that it exists. The seal is a Chaum-Pedersen proof of
discrete-log equality, made non-interactive and bound to a live gate
session.

You arrive with nothing, so the gate stamps you an envoy. No one is issued
a Khan tablet, and the Khan pairing is a hard discrete log away.

The scheme is published in full at `/docs`, and the reference is attached.
Open the Khan archive anyway.
