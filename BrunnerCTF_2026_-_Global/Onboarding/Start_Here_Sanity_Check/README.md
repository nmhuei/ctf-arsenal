### Welcome to BrunnerCTF 2026

Dear interns in Brunnerne Inc.™ - before starting your internship with us, here is some important introductory information, especially if you are new to CTFs

### What is a CTF?

A CTF (Capture The Flag) is a cybersecurity competition with challenges in areas like web exploitation, pwn, reverse engineering, cryptography, digital forensics, boot2root, and more.

Each challenge hides a flag in the format `brunner{something}`, often in [1337](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leet). Submit flags to earn points.

### Onboarding

You are currently in `Onboarding` - beginner-friendly challenges worth 10-40 points each. These challenges are tagged with the actual category.

If you get stuck with an `Onboarding` challenge, you can request hints on Discord via a `Hint` ticket - but always force yourself to really "try harder" first. No hints will be provided for other challenges.

### Dynamic Scoring

All other challenges are scored *dynamically*: the more solves a challenge has, the less it is worth for everyone who has solved it. Due to this, you might see your score decrease during the CTF.

Challenges start at a 1000 points and gradually decrease in score. After a specific number of solves, they reach a minimum of 100 points.

### Instanced Challenges

Some challenges only have a downloadable handout file. Others require you to connect to a website or server. Most of these have a "Start Challenge" button that starts your team's instance of that challenge. Only your team has access to this instance.

### Let's Go!

Before you move on, please read the [rules](/rules) of the CTF carefully. They contain important notes on what you're allowed to and not.

Then, submit the following flag:  
`brunner{welcome_to_your_new_unpaid_internship}`
